About

Why Acadex exists

Acadex is a student companion application designed to make academic life more organized and manageable. It brings together class schedules, subject information, academic activities, reminders, campus maps, and online announcements in one place.

Built to give students a convenient way to keep track of their academic responsibilities and stay informed throughout the semester. Acadex is developed by Kalabasaa as a tool to help him and other students manage their schedules, tasks, and reminders.

Follow Kalabasaa on GitHub for more projects, updates, and future improvements: github.com/kalabasaa

Install

How to install

Six steps on your Android phone.

  1. Open the Assets section

    Tap the download button above. On the GitHub release page, scroll down to Assets.

  2. Download the APK

    Tap acadex-v2.1.apk. If your browser warns that this type of file can be harmful, choose to keep it.

  3. Open the downloaded file

    Open the APK from your browser's download notice or from the Downloads folder in your Files app.

  4. Let your phone scan it

    Your phone checks the app for viruses. Let it scan. Acadex has nothing to hide.

  5. Allow installation if asked

    If your phone asks, allow installing apps from this source. You can turn it off again after installing.

  6. Tap Install

    Tap Install, then Open. You're all set.

Acadex is still under development. Some features may change or receive further improvements in future updates.

Docs

Using Acadex

Short guides for each part of the app. Tap a topic to open it.

Getting started
  1. Open Settings and type your name. The Home page will greet you with it.
  2. Add your subjects first. Classes and tasks belong to a subject.
  3. Add each weekly class, then your tasks and reminders.

The blue + button lets you add a subject, a schedule, a task or a reminder. The icons at the bottom switch between Home, Calendar, Map, Reminders and Settings. The icon of the page you are on turns blue.

Add a subject

Tap +, then Add subject. The subject name and code are required, and every code must be different. The professor is optional.

Tap a subject on Home to see its classes and tasks, edit it, or delete it. Deleting a subject also deletes its classes.

Set your weekly class schedule

Tap +, then Add schedule, or use + Add inside a subject.

  1. Pick the subject and the day.
  2. Pick the start and end time. The end must be later than the start.
  3. Choose Onsite and enter the room, or choose Online and enter the link or platform.

Each entry is one weekly meeting. A subject that meets on Monday and Wednesday needs two entries. Today's classes appear on Home.

Track assignments, projects, quizzes and exams

Tap +, then Add task. Choose a subject (optional), write a title, and pick the type: Assignment, Project, Quiz or Exam.

Add a due date and, if you want, a due time and a description. A task starts as Pending. Open it any time to change its status to In Progress, Finished or Missed.

Use the calendar

Tap a date to see the classes that meet on that weekday and the tasks due on that date. Tap Show all to list every task.

Tap a task to open its details, where you can read the full description, change its status or edit it.

Reminders and announcements

The Reminders page shows tomorrow's schedule, the tasks still to be finished, and online announcements from the school.

You can also add your own reminder with +. Pick a class or a task, write a title, and choose a date and time. Use the switch on a reminder to turn it on or off.

Find places on campus

Open Map to see the campus map. Pinch to zoom, drag to move around, and double-tap to zoom in or out.

Below the map, search the campus locations and guide, or look up what a college abbreviation stands for.

Import a schedule with an AI app

Acadex does not use AI itself. This feature lets you borrow any AI app to type your schedule for you.

  1. Open Settings and tap Copy AI prompt.
  2. Open any AI app. Paste the prompt and attach a clear photo or screenshot of your schedule.
  3. The AI replies with SQL. Copy only the SQL code block.
  4. Back in Acadex, paste it into the box and tap Check. Nothing is saved yet.
  5. If the check passes, tap Import.

If Check shows an error, tap Copy error for AI, send it to the AI, and paste the corrected SQL. Acadex only accepts commands that add subjects, classes and tasks, and it rejects anything else.

For better results, use a sharp, straight image with every column visible, and crop out everything that is not the schedule. Importing twice adds your classes twice, so delete your schedules first if you want to start over.

Delete your schedules or tasks

In Settings you can clear your data. Both actions ask you to confirm, and neither can be undone.

  • Delete all schedules removes every subject, class schedule and task, plus their reminders.
  • Delete all tasks removes only tasks and their reminders. Your subjects and class schedules stay.
Troubleshooting
My phone will not let me install the app.
Allow installing apps from the source you downloaded it with, such as your browser or Files app. The prompt usually has a Settings button that takes you there.
My phone says it is scanning the app.
That is the built-in virus check. Wait for it to finish, then continue.
Do I need internet to use Acadex?
No. Your schedule is saved on your phone. Only online announcements need a connection.
I found a problem or have a suggestion.
Acadex is still growing and feedback is welcome. Message Kalabasaa through GitHub.
